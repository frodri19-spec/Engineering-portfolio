A6: Bracket Drawing (part 1)

For this assignment I continued designing the bracket by taking the modeling from last week to create a parametric model and a detailed engineering drawing. I will be using the dimensions found during the stiffness analysis of the bracket because they were larger values than the stiffness analysis dimensions

Parametric design 

Started by inputting all of my equations for my stiffness analysis into SolidWorks

 

 

Now I just had to create my drawing 

Drawing

I put a construction line across my top view to show a possible parting line when manufacturing the bracket, I also put a construction line on the part the strap would go on to clearly show the shape of it .

Lessons learned 

This was my first time making drawings in SolidWorks, and the process turned out easier than I expected. I had to figure out where the tolerance settings were and learn the difference between third-angle and first-angle projection, but once I got that down the rest went smoothly. Overall, it showed me that with a little practice the tools aren’t too hard to use and drawings can be set up quickly.
