A3: Parametric and FEA

For this assignment I was tasked with designing a bar with a hollow box beam with an applied between 250 lbf and 500 lbf, with the max axial deflection of the bar being .01 inches and with the material being  Gray Cast Iron with a young modulus ranging from 11.6 to 21.7 x 10^ 6

First I listed my knowns and unknowns, and sketched my design. I decided to my box 1 x 1 inches and used .25 of an inch for my T value. Using these values I could solve for the length and are using the direct tension elongation equation

Now I could solve for Area and Length using parametric modeling and design and extrude my beam!

1 inch= 25.40mm

.25 of an inch= 6.35 mm

Next I could conduct a FEA analysis on my beam, I fixed one side of the beam and applied a force of 375 lbf ( 1668 Newtons) to the other side and conducted my test!

I was able to get the following results for the deflection and the von Mises stress graphs 

My maximum deflection was about 0.017319 mm which converts to about 0.0006819 inches which meets the requirements of .01 inches. My max stress came out to be about 4.603 × 10⁶ pascals which comes to be around 0.6676 ksi which is way below our max stress of 50 ksi 

And to calculate the safety factor I used SF= Material strength/ Max stress which comes out to be around 74.85 which is extremely safe compared to my materials strength
