Introduction

This week we have the opportunity to create and FEA analysis on the bracket we designed a couple assignments ago, as well as using FEA analysis to make a bracket that strong, stiff, and as lightweight as possible. Design 2

For this design I decided to make feature B a little thicker since that's where most of my failure points were

Instead of the previous .455 inches we calculated before I will be using .8 inches for the thickness of feature B which makes our total weight .84 pounds with that being said we can begin our analysis

Design 3

According to the results of my second design making the feature thicker will not keep the deflection and stress at a minimum, it actually maximizes it so for my third design I will be going back to my original thickness for feature B and add supports for it to help minimize deflection and help alleviate stress. Th weight will be the same .84 pounds
