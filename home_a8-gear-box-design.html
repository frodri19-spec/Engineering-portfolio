A8: Gear Box design

Introduction 

For this assignment we had the privilege of designing parts of a gearbox that can be easily assembled and disassembled, as well as design journal bearings that support the shafts.

 

Design of the shafts

First I had to check if diameters of .125 inches and .25 inches was enough to withstand the forces applied, according to the machinery's handbook the mac allowable shear strength should be less than 4000 psi for power transmitting shafts, and less than 8500 psi for short shafts 

My shear stress values came out to be 1224 psi for the input shaft and 408 psi for the output shaft which are both way below our allowable shear stress, this means our dimensions for our shafts will be functional on our gearbox!

 

Design of journal bearings 

We were told to choose an appropriate size and length of the journal bearing to determine the pressure, velocity and PV rating. With that being said I ended up choosing .35 inches for the length of my bearings which seemed like a nice size to fit in my gear box.

Using the equations from the machinery's handbook I started solving for the output bearing first 

 

Now for the Journal bearing of the input shaft 

 

Now with my calculations being done I can choose a material for the bearings using the table below provided during lecture 

 

Since my values sit just above 3000 PV the material to use for the bearings would be graphite.

 

Thickness of journal bearings 

Solving for the thickness was actually quite simple, all I had to do was derive the normal stress formula and solve for thickness

 

I ended up using the graphite High Temp lubricated sleeve bearing with a 1/4 shaft diameter and 3/8 housing for the output shaft and the High Temp graphite lubricated sleeve bearing with a 1/8 shaft diameter and a 1/4 housing (more details in the resources)

Lessons learned 

This gearbox project was my first real experience working with a complete mechanical assembly, and it taught me a lot about how individual parts come together to form a functional system. Going through the process of aligning shafts, gears, and bearings made me realize how important precision and design intent are when modeling components. It also gave me a better understanding of constraints, mates, and how tolerances affect motion in assemblies. Overall, this project helped me appreciate the level of detail and patience required in mechanical design and showed me how crucial assembly modeling skills are for real-world engineering work. This assignment took me about 9 hours
